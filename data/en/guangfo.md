# 🌉 Guangfo Line — The City Bridge!

> 🌉 **Fun Fact:** The Guangfo Line connects TWO different cities — **Guangzhou** (广州) and **Foshan** (佛山)! "Guangfo" is made from the first characters of both city names: 广+佛 = 广佛!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Yellow-Green |
| 🔢 Stations | **32 stations** |
| 🌆 Special | Crosses **two cities** in one journey! |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Xilang** — Transfer hub in Guangzhou (also Lines 1 & 7)!
- 🛣️ **Kuiqi Road** — Key stop in Foshan!
- 🏙️ **Foshan** — The heart of Foshan city!

## 🌟 Something Special
Guangzhou and Foshan are growing **so close together** that they're almost becoming one giant city! The Guangfo Line helps millions of people commute between the two cities every day — without it, the roads would be impossibly crowded. Two cities, one metro line, millions of happy travelers! 🎉

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Foshan is world-famous for **Kung Fu** 🥋! The legendary Bruce Lee (李小龙) had family roots in Foshan, and the equally famous Wong Fei-hung (黄飞鸿) was also from Foshan. So riding to Foshan is like entering the home of Kung Fu masters!
