# 🌿 Line 7 — The Scholar's Route!

> 📚 **Fun Fact:** Line 7 connects the university area to the big train station in the south — perfect for students traveling around the city!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Light Green |
| 🔢 Stations | **14 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | North–West to South |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Xilang** — Transfer point to Lines 1 and Guangfo Line!
- 🎓 **University Town North** — Enter the island of universities!
- 🚄 **Guangzhou South Railway Station** — Off to other cities!

## 🌟 Something Special
Line 7 is like a **shortcut bridge** connecting the university island to the high-speed rail hub in the south. Students can go from class to catching a fast train in no time! ⚡📖

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Did you know that University Town island has its own **free shuttle buses** that loop around? So you can take Line 7 there, then hop on a campus bus to explore all the different universities. It's like a mini-city inside a city! 🏙️

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Xilang (West) → University Town North → ... → Guangzhou South Railway Station
```
Green like fresh leaves — this line is all about learning and going places!
