# 🔵 Foshan Metro Line 3 — The Culture Connector!

## Quick Intro

Foshan Metro Line 3 is a dark blue metro line running north to south through Foshan City.
The first section opened on December 28, 2022, linking parts of Shunde District to central Foshan.
A second section opened on August 23, 2024, extending the line further north and south.
When fully built, Line 3 will stretch about 69.5 kilometers with around 37 to 38 stations.

## Story Time

Foshan is made up of several districts, and for a long time, getting between them took a long time by bus or car.
Shunde in the south and Nanhai in the north were far from each other by road.
City planners wanted to build a long line that could be the "backbone" of the whole Foshan metro system.
They also wanted the line to connect with Foshan West Railway Station, so that people arriving by high-speed train could easily reach all parts of the city.

The government approved the plan in 2012.
Construction started in 2016 and continued for several years.
Because the line is so long, it was built and opened in stages.
The first section opened on December 28, 2022.
A second section opened on August 23, 2024, with more parts still to come.

Line 3 still matters today because it is quickly becoming the busiest and most important line in Foshan.
After the 2024 opening, daily ridership jumped to over 160,000 passengers.

## Time Story

- **2012**: The government approved the plan for Line 3.
- **2016**: Construction officially started.
- **2022**: The first section opened on December 28, connecting parts of Shunde District to central Foshan.
- **2024**: A second section opened on August 23, extending the line north toward Foshan University and south toward Zhongshan Park.

Line 3 is still growing and becoming the backbone of Foshan's metro network.

## Challenges Along the Way

- **Building near a working railway**: Foshan West Railway Station is a huge intercity railway hub. Digging underground right next to it, while trains were still running, required very careful engineering and safety planning.
- **Working in a crowded city**: Most of the line runs underground in a dense urban area. Workers had to manage soft soil and groundwater, and protect buildings and roads above ground.
- **Opening in phases**: The line is nearly 70 kilometers long. It had to open in sections over several years, which meant teams needed to keep building new sections while earlier sections were already carrying passengers.
- **Cultural station design**: Several stations were themed with Foshan's traditional arts — dragon boats, lion dances, and Cantonese opera. Adding these special designs took extra time and required working with cultural experts.

## Route Snapshot

- **Start area**: Shunde College Railway Station (顺德学院站) area in the south, Shunde District
- **End area**: Foshan University (佛山大学) in Nanhai District to the north
- **Route role**: A long north-south backbone line connecting Shunde, Chancheng, and Nanhai districts
- **Transfer value**: Connects to the Guangfo Line at Guicheng and Dongping stations, to Foshan Line 2 at Wanhua, and to Guangzhou Metro Line 7 at Beijiao Park

## Important Stations for Kids

### 1) Foshan West Railway Station (佛山西站)

This is a large and important station where Line 3 connects with intercity and high-speed trains.
Trains from many different cities arrive and depart here.
Taking Line 3 to this station means you can quickly get on a fast train heading far away.

### 2) Guicheng (桂城)

Guicheng is a busy station in Nanhai District where you can transfer to the Guangfo Line.
The Guangfo Line connects Foshan and Guangzhou, so this station helps join two cities together.
It is a great example of how different metro lines can work as a team.

### 3) Daliang Zhonglou (大良中楼)

This station is located in the heart of Shunde District.
Shunde is famous for its food, its schools, and its history of craftsmanship and trade.
The station area reflects the cultural richness of this part of Foshan.

## Fun Facts

- Line 3 will be about **69.5 kilometers** long when fully completed.
- It will have around **37 to 38 stations**.
- Stations are decorated with art themes from Foshan culture, including **dragon boats**, **lion dances**, and **Cantonese opera**.
- After the August 2024 opening, daily ridership reached over **160,000 passengers**.

## Word Helper

- **Backbone line**: A main metro line that connects many important areas across a whole city, like a backbone holding everything together.
- **Intercity rail**: Trains that travel between cities, usually faster than local buses.
- **Cantonese opera**: A traditional form of musical storytelling from Guangdong province, with singing, colorful costumes, and elaborate face paint.
