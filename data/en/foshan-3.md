# 🏯 Foshan Line 3 — The District Connector!

## 🌟 Something Special
**Foshan West Railway Station** is a huge hub where high-speed trains from all over China arrive and depart! Line 3 connects this station to the rest of Foshan, making it a vital link for travelers. It's like the front gate to the whole city! 🚪🌆

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Foshan is not just famous for pottery and kung fu — it's also one of the **biggest furniture manufacturing cities** in the world! 🪑 The Shunde and Lecong furniture markets here are ENORMOUS — larger than some entire towns! If your home has Chinese-made furniture, there's a good chance it came from Foshan!
