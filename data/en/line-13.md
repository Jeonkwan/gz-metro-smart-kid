# 🌾 Line 13 — The Eastern Frontier!

> 🌾 **Fun Fact:** Line 13 reaches out to **Zengcheng** — the eastern edge of Guangzhou, famous for its lychee fruit! 🍈 Zengcheng lychees are the most delicious in the world!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Yellow-Green |
| 🔢 Stations | **14 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | West → East to Zengcheng |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🎆 **Yuzhu** — Near the famous Canton Fair site — the world's biggest trade fair!
- 🌊 **Xinsha** — New Sand — by the river!
- 💧 **Shuixi** — Water West — a poetic name!

## 🌟 Something Special
Every year, the **Canton Fair (广交会)** near Yuzhu station attracts businesses from over **200 countries**! It's one of the most important trade events in the whole world, right here in Guangzhou. 🌍🤝

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Zengcheng is known as the **"Home of Lychee"** 🍈! The famous poet Su Dongpo (苏东坡) wrote about how delicious Lingnan lychees are over 1,000 years ago. Some lychee trees in Zengcheng are over 400 years old! Imagine a tree older than some countries!

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Yuzhu (West) → Xinsha → Shuixi → ... → Zengcheng (East) 🌾
```
Ride east into the countryside — where the lychees grow and the air is fresh!
