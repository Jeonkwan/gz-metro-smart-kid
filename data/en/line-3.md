# 🟠 Line 3 — The Longest Adventure!

> ✈️ **Fun Fact:** Line 3 is the **longest metro line** in Guangzhou! It even goes all the way to the **airport** — so you can take the metro to catch your plane!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Orange |
| 🔢 Stations | **36 stations** (including the Branch Line!) |
| 📏 Direction | North–South + Branch to Airport |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🚌 **Tianhe Coach Terminal** — Buses going everywhere!
- 🚄 **Guangzhou East** — Connects to fast trains!
- 🏃 **Tiyu Xilu** — Near sports and shopping!
- ✈️ **Airport North / Airport South** — Fly away! Bon voyage! 🛫

## 🌟 Something Special
Line 3 has a **special branch** — the train splits at one point and some trains go to the Airport while others go to Panyu in the south. It's like a train that can choose two different paths! 🛤️🛤️

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
The airport stations are called **"T1" and "T2"** (Terminal 1 and Terminal 2) of Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport. Millions of people fly in and out every year — and many of them ride Line 3 to get there! 🌍

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Panyu Square (South) → ... → Tiyu Xilu → ... → Airport North ✈️
                                     ↘ Branch → Tianhe Coach Terminal
```
Two endings, one awesome line!
