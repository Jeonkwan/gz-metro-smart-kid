# 🌸 Haizhu Tram — The Scenic Garden Ride!

> 🌸 **Fun Fact:** The Haizhu Tram is a lovely **slow tram** that runs through a greenway park in Haizhu District — so you're riding through a garden! 🌿🌺

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Green |
| 🔢 Stations | **7 stations** |
| 🚋 Type | Light Rail Tram (above ground!) |
| 🌿 Special | Runs through a **greenway park**! |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Wanshengwei** — Transfer to Lines 4 and 8!
- 🌺 **Ruibao East** — In the heart of Haizhu!
- 🌿 **Nanzhou** — Southern greenery!

## 🌟 Something Special
Unlike most metro trains that go underground, the Haizhu Tram runs **above ground** through a beautiful green park corridor! 🌳 People jog and cycle along the greenway next to the tracks. It's a peaceful and pretty ride — perfect for taking in the scenery!

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
The Haizhu Tram route was built along what used to be **old factory railway tracks** 🏭! People transformed the old industrial area into a beautiful green park with a tram — turning something old into something beautiful and useful. That's called **upcycling**! ♻️✨

Slow down and smell the flowers — the Haizhu Tram is a journey, not just a trip!
