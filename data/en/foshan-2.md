# 🏺 Foshan Line 2 — The Pottery Cruiser!

> 🏺 **Fun Fact:** Foshan is the home of **Shiwan Pottery** — some of the most famous ceramics in China! Shiwan pottery figures (like little animals and people made from clay) have been made here for over 1,000 years!

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🏙️ **Guicheng** — "Tortoise City" — sounds like a place from a legend! 🐢
- 🏛️ **Chancheng** — The main district of Foshan city!
- 🏺 **Shiwan** — Famous pottery town!

## 🌟 Something Special
**Chancheng** (禅城) means "Zen City" — and Foshan has a beautiful **Ancestral Temple (祖庙)** here that's over 900 years old! 🏛️ It's full of incredible dragon sculptures and traditional Chinese art. Every Lunar New Year, thousands of people come here to celebrate!

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
**Shiwan pottery dragons** 🐉 are incredibly detailed — master potters spend weeks making just one! These colorful clay dragons can be found decorating the roofs of traditional temples. Can you imagine a dragon on your roof?
