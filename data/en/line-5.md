## 🌟 Something Special
Zhujiang New Town station puts you right in the middle of Guangzhou's **Opera House**, **Museum**, and **Library** — all in one neighborhood! You can learn, watch a show, and ride the metro all in one day! 🎭📚🎨

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
The Guangzhou Opera House near this line looks like two giant **pebbles** dropped by a river. One is called the "Sun Stone" and one is called the "Moon Stone." Doesn't that sound like something from a fairy tale? 🌙☀️
