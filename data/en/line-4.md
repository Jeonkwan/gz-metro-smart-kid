# 🟢 Line 4 — The University Explorer!

> 🎓 **Fun Fact:** Line 4 passes right by Guangzhou University Town — a special area with MANY universities all in one place! Lots of students ride this line every day!

| | |
|---|---|

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Wanshengwei** — Transfer to Line 8!
- 🎓 **University Town South** — Right in the middle of the university area!
- 🏝️ **Jinzhou** — Close to the southern coast near Nansha!

## 🌟 Something Special
Line 4 goes all the way to **Nansha** — a brand-new area being built in the far south of Guangzhou! Nansha is like a city of the future, with shiny new buildings and a port where huge ships dock. 🚢

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Guangzhou University Town (near the University Town South station) is on an island in the Pearl River! 🏝️ It was built specially for students and has 10 different universities! Imagine living on a school island — how cool is that?

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Wanshengwei → University Town South → ... → Jinzhou (Nansha)
```
From the city center, through the university island, all the way to the future city of Nansha!
