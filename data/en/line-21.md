# 🌑 Guangzhou Metro Line 21 — The Eastern Express!

## Quick Intro

Line 21 is a major express metro line connecting central Guangzhou to the eastern district of Zengcheng.
It connects Tianhe Park (天河公园) in the city center to Zengcheng Square (增城广场) in the east.
The line was opened in two stages, with the full route completed on December 20, 2019.
Today, Line 21 has 20 stations and is about 61.5 kilometers long.

## Story Time

Before Line 21, the eastern parts of Guangzhou felt very far from the city centre.
Zengcheng — a large area in the east — had only slow bus routes connecting it to the rest of Guangzhou.
A bus trip could take more than an hour.
Zengcheng became a district of Guangzhou in 2014, so the city needed a fast link to bring it closer.

Along the route, a place called Science City (科学城) in Huangpu District was growing into a major technology hub.
Companies and researchers were moving there, but the journey from the city centre was still slow.

City planners decided to build Line 21 as an eastern corridor metro.
Construction crews tunneled through the hilly and complex terrain between Tianhe and Zengcheng.
When the full line opened on December 20, 2019, the journey from the city center to Zengcheng Square (增城广场) became much quicker.

Line 21 still matters today because thousands of workers, students, and families in the eastern suburbs use it every day to reach the city centre.

## Time Story

- **2018**: The first section from Zhenlong (镇龙) to Zengcheng Square (增城广场) opened on December 28.
- **2019**: The full line from Tianhe Park (天河公园) to Zengcheng Square (增城广场) opened on December 20.

Line 21 transformed the journey to Zengcheng, offering both regular and express train services to make the long trip much faster.

## Challenges Along the Way

- **Long distance through hilly ground**: Line 21 had to stretch across one of the longest routes in the eastern suburbs. The terrain between Tianhe and Zengcheng includes hills and harder rock, which made tunneling more difficult than in the flat city centre.
- **Crossing through busy districts**: Much of the route runs beneath busy roads and residential areas in Huangpu District. Workers had to tunnel carefully to avoid disrupting homes, roads, and existing underground pipes above the tunnels.
- **Building a new terminus in an undeveloped area**: The eastern end — Zengcheng Square (增城广场) — was still being developed when the line was planned. Engineers had to build the station and depot as the new town grew around it.

## Route Snapshot

- **Start area**: Tianhe Park (天河公园) — western terminus in Tianhe District
- **End area**: Zengcheng Square (增城广场) — eastern terminus in Zengcheng District
- **Route role**: High-speed eastern corridor connecting the city center, Science City (科学城), and Zengcheng
- **Transfer value**: Connects with Line 11 and Line 13 at Tianhe Park (天河公园); Line 4 at Huangcun (Transfer to Line 21); Line 6 at Suyuan (Transfer to Line 21); Line 7 at Shuixi (水西); and Line 14 at Zhenlong (镇龙)

## Important Stations for Kids

### 1) Tianhe Park (天河公园)

This is the western end of Line 21.
Tianhe Park (天河公园) is a large, green park in the heart of Guangzhou.
Many families come here on weekends to walk, play, and enjoy nature.
After your metro ride, you can go for a stroll in the park!

### 2) Science City Area (科学城)

Along the middle of the route, the line passes through Guangzhou's Science City (科学城) in Huangpu District.
This is a special zone where technology companies, universities, and researchers work together.
Think of it as the neighbourhood where clever people invent things and build the future!

### 3) Zengcheng Square (增城广场)

This is the eastern end of the line.
Zengcheng Square (增城广场) is a large public area in the heart of Zengcheng District.
It is surrounded by shops, libraries, and government buildings, making it a very important hub for the local community.

## Fun Facts

- Line 21 was one of the first in Guangzhou to offer "express" trains that skip smaller stations to get you to the end faster.
- The line colour is a deep purple, standing out in the metro map.
- Tianhe Park (天河公园) Station is one of the largest underground metro stations in Asia!

## Word Helper

- **Express service**: A train that skips some stations to arrive at the destination more quickly.
- **Corridor**: A major transport route that connects several important areas.
- **Asia's Largest**: Tianhe Park (天河公园) station is so big it can fit several regular stations inside it!
