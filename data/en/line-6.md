## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🏛️ **Dongshan Kou** — One of Guangzhou's most charming old neighborhoods!
- 🎨 **Cultural Park** — Art, culture, and history right at your doorstep!
- 🌿 **Changban** — Eastern terminus!
- 🌊 **Bijiang** — Near the riverside!

## 🌟 Something Special
Dongshan Kou (East Mountain Mouth) is famous for its **beautiful old Republican-era buildings** — the kind with big windows, arched doors, and colorful tiles. Walking around there feels like stepping back in time! 🏚️✨
