# 🎠 Nanhai Tram Line 1 — The Gentle Blue Ride!

> 🎠 **Fun Fact:** Nanhai Tram Line 1 is a soft, light-blue tram in **Nanhai District** of Foshan — like a gentle wave from the sea! 🌊

| | |
|---|---|

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🌆 **Xialang** — "Lower Wave" — a beautiful riverside name!
- 🏙️ **Guicheng** — The center of Nanhai District!

## 🌟 Something Special
Nanhai (南海) means **"South Sea"** — and this light-blue tram feels like a gentle breeze from the ocean! 🌊 It helps residents of Nanhai get around the district easily and connects them to the bigger metro network.

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
**Nanhai** has been a famous place for over **2,000 years**! In ancient times, ships from the "Nanhai" (South Sea) brought goods from Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and even Africa to China. Imagine all the amazing things those ancient ships carried! 🐘🌴🏺
