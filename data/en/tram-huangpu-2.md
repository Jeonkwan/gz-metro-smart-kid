## 🌟 Something Special
This tram serves the **newer parts of Huangpu District** — a growing area with technology parks, research centers, and modern neighborhoods! It connects communities so that people can easily get around without needing a car. 🚗➡️🚋

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Did you notice that **Tangtou** (棠头) sounds like "candy head" 🍭? The real meaning is "pond head" — a place where there used to be ponds. But we like the sweet candy version better! Maybe there's candy hiding somewhere in Tangtou...
