# 🔵 Line 2 — The Big Station Connector!

> 🚄 **Fun Fact:** Line 2 connects TWO of Guangzhou's biggest train stations — so you can travel to almost anywhere in China from this line!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Blue |
| 🔢 Stations | **24 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | North ↕ South |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🚄 **Guangzhou South Railway Station** — One of the BIGGEST train stations in the whole world! High-speed trains zoom off from here!
- 🏛️ **Haizhu Square** — A famous square in the city center!
- ✈️ **Sanyuanli** — Close to the old airport area, full of history!

## 🌟 Something Special
Line 2 is like a **spine** running through the city from north to south. It makes it super easy to travel from the top of the city all the way to the bottom! 🗺️

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Guangzhou South Railway Station (on Line 2) is so big that it has **28 platforms**! That's like having 28 different roads where trains can park. It's one of the busiest stations in all of China! 😮

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Jiahewanggang (North) → ... → Haizhu Square → ... → Guangzhou South Railway Station (South)
```
From the north of the city all the way down to the south — Line 2 has you covered!
