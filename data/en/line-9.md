# 🌿 Guangzhou Metro Line 9 — Into Huadu!

## Quick Intro

Line 9 is the first metro line to reach Huadu District in northern Guangzhou.
It opened on December 28, 2017, after years of careful planning and difficult construction.
The line runs west to east, connecting Fei'eling to Gaozeng, where it meets Line 3.
Today, Line 9 has 11 stations and is about 20.1 kilometers long.

## Story Time

Before Line 9, people living in Huadu District had no metro at all.
Huadu sits about 40 kilometers north of Guangzhou's city center.
Getting downtown meant taking a bus or a long car ride.
When planners announced a new metro line for Huadu in 2009, the community was excited.
But building Line 9 turned out to be much harder than expected.
The ground in Huadu is full of karst caves — hidden holes inside limestone formed by millions of years of rain and water.
Engineers had to redesign the line so it would go entirely underground, to keep passengers safe from unstable ground.
After years of careful work, Line 9 opened on December 28, 2017.
It was a big day for Huadu — for the first time, the district had its own metro connection to the city.

## Time Story

- **2009**: Environmental assessment announced in July; construction began in September.
- **2012**: Original planned opening date — missed because of route redesign needed for karst terrain.
- **2017**: Line 9 opened on December 28, with 10 stations serving Huadu District.
- **2018**: Qingtang, an infill station, opened on June 30.

Line 9 gave Huadu District its first metro connection to the rest of Guangzhou.
It helped the area grow as more people could now travel easily between Huadu and the city center.

## Challenges Along the Way

- **Karst caves underground**: Builders found up to 98 hidden limestone caves along the tunnel route. Each cave had to be filled or carefully worked around so the tunnels would not sink or collapse.
- **Crossing a high-speed railway**: Line 9 became the first metro in China to tunnel beneath a high-speed railway track. Engineers had to be extremely precise, because even a small shift in the ground could damage the rail lines above.
- **Full redesign needed**: The line was originally planned to have elevated sections above ground. But the karst ground was too unstable for surface or elevated tracks. The whole route had to be redesigned to go underground, which delayed the opening by five years.

## Route Snapshot

- **Start area**: Fei'eling (飞鹅岭), in the western part of Huadu District
- **End area**: Gaozeng (高增), in the east, connecting to Line 3
- **Route role**: An east-west line serving Huadu District, linking industrial zones, residential areas, and a major railway station
- **Transfer value**: Connects to Line 3 at Gaozeng, giving riders access to the broader Guangzhou metro network

## Important Stations for Kids

### 1) Guangzhou North Railway Station (广州北站)

This is one of the most important stations on Line 9.
It links the metro with intercity and high-speed trains going to Qingyuan and other cities in Guangdong.
Passengers can switch between the metro and long-distance trains here.
It makes Huadu a gateway between Guangzhou and northern Guangdong.

### 2) Huadu Square (花都广场)

Huadu Square station is near the center of Huadu District.
It is close to the district government offices and main shopping areas.
It is one of the busiest stops on Line 9 for people living in and visiting Huadu.
The name "Huadu" (花都) means "City of Flowers" in Chinese.

### 3) Gaozeng (高增)

Gaozeng is the eastern terminus of Line 9.
It is the most important interchange station on this line.
From here, riders can switch to Line 3 and travel to Guangzhou Baiyun International Airport or to Tianhe in the city center.
Gaozeng is the link that ties Line 9 to the whole Guangzhou metro network.

## Fun Facts

- Line 9 is the **first metro line in China** to be built entirely in karst terrain.
- Workers found up to **98 karst caves** along the tunnel route.
- The line was originally planned to open in **2012** but opened five years later in **2017** because of underground cave challenges.
- Line 9 is about **20.1 kilometers** long with **11 stations**, all underground.

## Word Helper

- **Karst**: A type of ground formed when rainwater slowly dissolves limestone over millions of years, leaving behind caves and hollow spaces.
- **High-speed railway subgrade**: The strong base that a high-speed train track sits on. Tunneling beneath it without disturbing it is very difficult.
- **Infill station**: A station added to an existing line after it opens, placed between two existing stations.
