# 🌱 Line 9 — The Huadu Explorer!

> 🌿 **Fun Fact:** Line 9 goes all the way to **Huadu District** — a beautiful northern area of Guangzhou famous for its flowers! Huadu even hosts the famous China (Guangzhou) International Garden Fair! 🌺

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Light Green |
| 🔢 Stations | **10 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | Into Huadu from the south |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🦅 **Fei'eling** — "Flying Ling" — what a cool name!
- 🏙️ **Huadu Square** — The center of Huadu District!
- 🌸 **Xinhua** — A lovely town with gardens nearby!

## 🌟 Something Special
Huadu is known as the **"City of Flowers"** 🌸 in Guangdong! There are huge flower markets and gardens, and every year people come from all over to see the beautiful blooms. Line 9 is your ticket to the flower city!

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
In Chinese, 花都 (Huadu) literally means "Flower Capital"! 🌷 Imagine living in a place officially named after flowers — every day would feel like spring!

```
Fei'eling → ... → Huadu Square → Xinhua
```
Short but sweet — like a little garden path through the flower district!
