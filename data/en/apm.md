# 🤖 APM Line — The Robot Train!

> 🤖 **Fun Fact:** The APM Line is a **driverless automatic train** — there's NO driver at the front! A computer controls everything! It's like riding a very smart robot! 🦾

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Light Blue |
| 🔢 Stations | **9 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | A loop/line through Zhujiang New Town |
| 🤖 Special | Fully **automatic & driverless**! |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Linhexi** — Transfer to other metro lines!
- 🏙️ **Guang Zhou Da Dao South** — Guangzhou Boulevard South — heart of the city!
- 🏟️ **Tianhe Sports Center** — The sports complex and stadium!

## 🌟 Something Special
APM stands for **Automated People Mover** — it's basically a people-moving robot! The trains are smaller and lighter than regular metro trains, and they glide silently on rubber wheels. If you stand at the front of the train, you can see the tunnel ahead with NO driver in the way! 🌟

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
The APM Line is a bit like the **people-mover trains** you find in airports (like the ones that connect different terminals). It was specially designed for **Zhujiang New Town** — Guangzhou's ultra-modern business district. The futuristic trains match the futuristic buildings! 🏙️🤖

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Linhexi → ... → Guang Zhou Da Dao South → ... → Tianhe Sports Center
```
Light blue like the sky — and as smart as a computer!
