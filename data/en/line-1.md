# 🟡 Line 1 — The Pioneer!

> 🏆 **Fun Fact:** Line 1 was the **very first** metro line in Guangzhou, opened all the way back in **1997**! It's the grandpa of all Guangzhou metro lines!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Yellow |
| 🔢 Stations | **16 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | East ↔ West through central Guangzhou |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🏁 **Xilang** — The western starting point!
- 🚄 **Guangzhou East Railway Station** — Hop on a fast train to other cities!
- 🏟️ **Tianhe Sports Center** — A huge sports arena right next to the station!

## 🌟 Something Special
Line 1 runs right through the **heart of Guangzhou city**! It was so important that it helped build the whole metro network we have today. Every other line came AFTER Line 1 showed the way. 🎉

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
When Line 1 first opened, it only had a **few trains** running. Now the trains come every few minutes! Imagine waiting a long time just to ride the metro — things have changed a LOT! 😄

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Xilang → ... → Gongyuanqian → ... → Guangzhou East Railway Station
```
It zips across the middle of Guangzhou, connecting the west side to the east side!
