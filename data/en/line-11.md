# ✨ Line 11 — The Ring Line!

> ⭕ **Fun Fact:** Line 11 is a **ring line** — that means it goes in a big circle around the city center, like a giant hula hoop for trains! 🌀

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Orange / Gold |
| 🔢 Stations | **8 stations** (ring section) |
| 📏 Direction | Circular loop |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🚂 **Guangzhou Railway Station** — The grand old railway station, opened in 1974!
- 🚌 **Tianhe Coach Terminal** — Buses and coaches to everywhere!
- ⛪ **Chigang** — Historical area in the east of the old city!

## 🌟 Something Special
Ring lines are super special because **you can go around in a circle** — there's no "end" station! If you miss your stop, just keep going and you'll come back around. It's like a merry-go-round for the city! 🎠

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Guangzhou Railway Station (not the South one!) was built in 1974 and is shaped like a grand palace 🏰. It was once one of the busiest train stations in China! Now Line 11 connects it to the rest of the modern metro network.

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
🔄 → Guangzhou Railway Station → Tianhe Coach Terminal → Chigang → 🔄
```
Around and around — the golden ring of Guangzhou's metro!
