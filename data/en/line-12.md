# 🍵 Line 12 — The Diagonal Dream!

## Quick Intro

Line 12 is Guangzhou's bold diagonal metro line.
It cuts across the city from the northwest all the way to the southeast.
It is tea-green in colour and connects four big districts in one ride.
It is also Guangzhou's first big driverless metro line — no driver in the cab at all!

## Story Time

Long ago, getting from Baiyun District in the northwest to the University Town in the southeast was very hard.
You had to take many different trains and change lines several times.
Each transfer added time, and the journey felt very long.

City planners wanted to fix that.
In 2017, the government approved a new line — Line 12 — to cut diagonally across Guangzhou.
Work began on 19 November 2018.

Building a diagonal line through a busy city is not easy.
Workers dug tunnels under busy roads, under the Pearl River, and even under a famous island called Ersha Island.
The central section of the line was very difficult to build, so it could not open at the same time as the rest.

On 29 June 2025, two sections opened together — the west section and the east section.
More than 164,000 passengers rode Line 12 on that first day alone.

Today, Line 12 helps hundreds of thousands of people cross the city faster every single day.
Together with Line 10, it forms a giant "X" shape across Guangzhou.

## Time Story

- **2017**: Government approves Line 12 as part of Guangzhou's third railway building plan.
- **19 November 2018**: Full-line construction officially begins.
- **2025, June 29**: West section and east section open together — 18 stations start running.
- **2026, February 13**: Chigang station opens, bringing more passengers on board.
- **Future**: The central section is still being built and will connect the two halves when it is ready.

Line 12 is still growing. When the central section opens, it will be one of the longest diagonal metro lines in China.

## Challenges Along the Way

- **Tunnelling under the Pearl River**: Workers had to dig tunnels under the Pearl River near Ersha Island. The ground there is soft and full of water. Digging in wet, soft ground is very risky because the tunnel walls can crack or water can leak inside. Engineers used special shield tunnelling machines and worked very carefully.

- **Building near a cultural island**: Ersha Island is home to the Xinghai Concert Hall and the Guangdong Art Museum — both very important places. Construction machinery can shake the ground. Too much shaking can crack old buildings. Engineers had to control every vibration very precisely to keep these places safe.

- **Learning to run without a driver**: Line 12 is the first big mainline metro in Guangzhou to run with no driver at all (called GoA4 automation). This is the highest level of automation. Before it could carry passengers, engineers had to test every computer system hundreds of times to make sure the trains would stop, start, and stay safe all by themselves.

- **Opening in pieces, not all at once**: Because the central section was so difficult, engineers could not open the whole line at the same time. They opened the west and east ends first. Running a line in two separate pieces required extra planning so that passengers would not get confused.

## Route Snapshot

- **Start area**: Xunfenggang (northwest, Baiyun District)
- **End area**: Higher Education Mega Center South (southeast, Panyu District)
- **Route role**: Diagonal connector linking Baiyun, Yuexiu, Haizhu, and Panyu Districts
- **Transfer value**: 17 transfer stations along the full route — the most of any Guangzhou metro line

## Important Stations for Kids

### 1) Xunfenggang (浔峰岗)

This is the northwest starting point of Line 12.
Here you can also change to Line 6.
"Xunfeng" (浔峰) is the name of an old area near this station, close to the Baiyun mountains.

### 2) Ersha Island (二沙岛)

Ersha Island is a beautiful island in the Pearl River in the middle of the city.
It is home to the Xinghai Concert Hall, where musicians perform great music, and the Guangdong Art Museum, full of paintings and sculptures.
Before Line 12 opened, Ersha Island had no metro station at all. Line 12 gave it its very first one!

### 3) Higher Education Mega Center South (大学城南)

This is in Guangzhou's famous University Town — a place where many universities share one campus area.
Thousands of students live and study here.
This station also connects to Lines 4 and 7, so students can get anywhere in the city easily.

## Fun Facts

- Line 12 is Guangzhou's first big mainline metro to run fully without a driver — it uses GoA4, the highest automation level for any metro in the world.
- On its opening day on 29 June 2025, over 164,000 people rode Line 12. By 31 December 2025, a single-day record of 319,000 riders was set.
- Line 12 has 17 transfer stations along its full route — more than any other Guangzhou metro line — earning it the nickname "the Transfer King" (换乘之王).
- Together with Line 10, Line 12 forms a giant "X" shape across the city, giving Guangzhou a unique diagonal metro backbone.

## Word Helper

- **GoA4 (Grade of Automation 4)**: This means the train runs all by itself — no driver needed. The computer controls everything. It is the highest level of metro automation.
- **Diagonal**: A direction that goes at a slant, not straight across and not straight up-down. Line 12 goes diagonally, from the northwest corner to the southeast corner of the city.
- **Shield tunnelling**: A way of digging tunnels using a big round machine that cuts through the ground and puts up strong walls at the same time. It keeps the tunnel from collapsing while workers are inside.
- **Transfer station**: A station where you can change from one metro line to another without going outside.
