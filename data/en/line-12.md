# 🌲 Line 12 — The Nansha Voyager!

> 🚢 **Fun Fact:** Line 12 goes all the way to **Nansha Passenger Port** — where you can board a ferry and sail across the sea to Macau or Hong Kong! 🌊

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Jiahe Wanggang** — Northern transfer hub!
- 🏰 **Longguicheng** — Dragon Tortoise City — what a fun name! 🐢🐉
- 🚢 **Nansha Passenger Port** — Board a ferry here for your sea adventure!

## 🌟 Something Special
At **Nansha Passenger Port**, you can take a high-speed ferry to **Macau** in about an hour, or to **Hong Kong** too! Imagine: subway + ferry = international adventure! 🌏✨

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
**Longguicheng** (龙归城) means "Dragon Returns to the City" 🐉! That sounds like the title of an epic adventure movie. Maybe there really are dragons hiding in Nansha... (just kidding! 😄)
