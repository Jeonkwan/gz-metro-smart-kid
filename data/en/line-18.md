# ⚡ Line 18 — The Lightning Express!

> ⚡ **Fun Fact:** Line 18 is a **super-fast express metro line** — it has fewer stops so it can go much faster between stations! It's one of the fastest metro lines in Guangzhou!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Dark Blue |
| 🔢 Stations | **7 stations** |
| 📏 Direction | North → South Express |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🌊 **Wanqingsha** — "Ten Thousand Green Sands" — in the southern Nansha area!
- 🏙️ **Panyu Square** — Center of Panyu District!
- 🚄 **Guangzhou East** — Fast connection to the east!

## 🌟 Something Special
Express metro lines like Line 18 skip many stations and zoom between the important ones at **super high speed**! Because there are only 7 stops, you can travel a huge distance in just a short time. It's like taking a rocket instead of a bus! 🚀

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
The trains on express lines like Line 18 can go up to **160 km per hour**! That's about twice as fast as regular city metro trains. If you look out the window, everything rushes by like a blur! 💨

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Wanqingsha (South) → Panyu Square → ... → Guangzhou East (North)
```
Dark blue like the night sky — fast, sleek, and exciting!
