# ⭐ Line 24 — The Northern Star!

> ✨ **Status:** Line 24 is currently **under construction** as of May 2026. It is expected to become a major north-south artery connecting the northern suburbs to the city center!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Cream / Light Yellow |
| 🔢 Stations | **18 stations** (planned) |
| 📏 Length | Approximately 31.7 km |
| 📏 Direction | Running from Huadu through Baiyun to Yuexiu |

## 🌟 Something Special
New metro lines like Line 24 are like **new roads being drawn** on the city map! When it opens, thousands of people living in the northern parts of Guangzhou will be able to travel to the city center much faster than by bus. It will connect two big railway stations: Guangzhou North and the new Guangzhou Baiyun Station. 🌟

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Building a metro line under a city is one of the **hardest construction jobs** in the world! Workers use giant tunnel-boring machines (TBMs) that are as big as a house 🏠, slowly drilling through rock and earth to carve out the underground tunnels where the trains will run. Amazing, right?
