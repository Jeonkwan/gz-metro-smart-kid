# ⭐ Line 24 — The Northern Star!

> ✨ **Fun Fact:** Line 24 is one of Guangzhou's **newest lines**, opening up northern parts of the city that didn't have metro access before! It's still growing — like a young star getting brighter!

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Cream / Light Yellow |
| 🔢 Stations | **15 stations** (planned/partial) |
| 📏 Direction | Running through northern Guangzhou |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🔄 **Tangxi** — Transfer station connecting to other lines!
- 🏘️ **Longkeng** — "Dragon's Pit" — sounds exciting! 🐉

## 🌟 Something Special
New metro lines like Line 24 are like **new roads being drawn** on the city map! When a new line opens, thousands of people who used to spend hours on buses can suddenly travel much faster and easier. A new metro line changes lives! 🌟

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
Building a metro line under a city is one of the **hardest construction jobs** in the world! Workers use giant tunnel-boring machines (TBMs) that are as big as a house 🏠, slowly drilling through rock and earth to carve out the underground tunnels where the trains will run. Amazing, right?

## 🗺️ Where Does It Go?
```
Tangxi → ... → Longkeng → ... (and growing! ✨)
```
Cream-colored like a blank page — this line's story is still being written!
