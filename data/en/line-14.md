# 🏔️ Line 14 — Into the Mountains!

> 🏔️ **Fun Fact:** Line 14 goes all the way to **Conghua** — a mountainous area famous for its hot springs and beautiful waterfalls. It's like a nature adventure on the metro! 🌿

## 🚉 Quick Facts
| | |
|---|---|
| 🎨 Color | Brown / Dark Red |
| 🔢 Stations | **26 stations** (including Knowledge City Line branch!) |
| 📏 Direction | City Center → Northeast to Conghua |

## ⭐ Famous Stations
- 🏙️ **Xinhe** — Transfer point in the eastern suburbs!
- 🌲 **Conghua** — Gateway to beautiful mountain scenery!
- 🧪 **Knowledge City** — A high-tech science and innovation zone! |

## 🌟 Something Special
The **Knowledge City (知识城)** branch of Line 14 goes to a brand-new science city being built in the northeast — it's where technology companies, universities, and research labs are all being built together! The city of the future! 🔬🚀

## 🎈 A Fun Fact for You!
**Conghua Hot Springs** (从化温泉) have been famous for over **1,000 years**! Even emperors and important people came to relax in the warm natural spring water. Today, families go there to relax in hot spring pools surrounded by mountains and green trees. So cozy! 🛁🌸
